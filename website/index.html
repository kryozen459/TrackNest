A few things to know

Questions,
answered.

Can TrackNest stream songs from the internet?+

TrackNest plays music files saved on your PC. Its downloader can save supported media links to a folder you choose.

What can I play in TrackNest?+

Add audio files from your PC to Your music. The player includes play/pause, previous/next, seeking, volume, shuffle, and repeat.

Can I use the app without installing it?+

The Windows trial is a portable ZIP. Extract the folder and open tracknest.exe to start.

Where do converted or downloaded files go?+

Choose a save folder in the app before converting or downloading media.
